# Success Center ERP

register: product

## Product Purpose
Arabic RTL admin and ops system for Success Educational Center: students, teachers, bookings, session check-in, finance, and parent portal. Powered by Cowdlly.

## Users
- Reception / center manager (daily ops, bookings, payments)
- Teachers (attendance, groups)
- Parents / students (portal)

## Brand
- Name: Success
- Primary: deep navy `#0B2545`
- Accent: warm gold `#C99612`
- Tone: clear, trustworthy, Arabic-first, operational (not flashy SaaS)

## Anti-references
- Purple gradients, neon dark dashboards, emoji-as-icons, identical generic card grids
- Symbol soup (◆ ◇ ◎) instead of real icons

## Principles
1. Task speed over decoration
2. One icon language (outline SVG) across nav and actions
3. Brand navy + gold stay dominant; semantic colors only for status
4. Preserve RTL and Cairo typography
