# Success Center — Design

## Scene
Bright daytime reception desk in an Egyptian educational center: staff scan lists and phones quickly. Light surfaces, calm navy structure, gold for emphasis.

## Color strategy
Committed navy shell + gold accent (~15%), with a small semantic palette for status (success / warn / danger / info / teal ops).

### Tokens (OKLCH-leaning hex)
- navy / navy-deep / navy-soft — structure, sidebar, headings
- gold / gold-soft / gold-deep — CTAs, active nav, highlights
- surface `#F4F7FB`, panel tinted white, mist borders
- semantic: emerald, amber, rose, sky, teal

## Typography
Cairo (400–800). Product scale ~1.15. No display font swap.

## Icons
Lucide outline, 1.75–2px stroke, consistent 18–20px in nav.

## Motion
150–220ms ease-out on hover/active only.
